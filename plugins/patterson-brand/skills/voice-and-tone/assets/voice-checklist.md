# Patterson voice check

Run before publishing.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Identify the applicable layer first — corporate always, plus Dental and/or social where relevant.

---

## Layer 1 — corporate, always in force `[BG25 p.10–12]`

- [ ] **Optimistic** — negatives rewritten as positives; solutions, not problems
- [ ] **Clear** — no unnecessary prepositional phrases, "ands" or "thats"
- [ ] No *great, special, really, very*
- [ ] One thought per sentence
- [ ] Adverbs replaced with the right word
- [ ] **Truthful** — no internal jargon; written *with* the customer, not *at* them
- [ ] **Personable** — sounds like a caring person wrote it; formality level fits the audience
- [ ] Active voice throughout
- [ ] AP style (AMA if it is a medical article)
- [ ] Scannable — clear headlines and subheads that state the intent
- [ ] One CTA; never more than three
- [ ] Sentence case everywhere
- [ ] No `Inc.` or `Co.`
- [ ] Brand promise correct for context — comma and no ® in copy; periods and ® in designed graphics,
      once per piece

## Layer 2 — Patterson Dental only `[VOICE18]`

- [ ] **Empathetic** — shows insider knowledge of how the practice feels; not overselling
- [ ] **Empowering** — points to the next step toward the customer's vision; nudges rather than shoves
- [ ] **Authoritative** — speaks with confidence about business practices, equipment, technology,
      software and merchandise; not shy
- [ ] Any "years in business" figure verified (the source document says 140+, written in 2018)
- [ ] Dental attributes **not** applied to Veterinary or AHI copy — no addendum exists for those

## Layer 3 — social only `[SOC19]`, `[BG25 p.47]`

- [ ] **First-person plural** — *we, us, our*. No *I*. No *they*.
- [ ] Educates, informs or entertains rather than selling
- [ ] Fits the **80/20** value-to-promotion split
- [ ] Reads like a polite host, not a boastful one
- [ ] Hashtags researched on every platform they will run on
- [ ] Hashtag count right for the platform — 2–3 on X/LinkedIn, up to 15 on Instagram, emphasis-only
      on Facebook
- [ ] Hashtags capitalize the first letter of each word
- [ ] No `#Patterson`
- [ ] Emoji, exclamation points and caps enhance rather than overpower
- [ ] Image is a photo of real people where possible; correct format (`.png` for graphics, `.jpg` for
      photos) and size (1200×628 and/or 1080×1080)

## Layer 4 — internal communications `[BG25 p.43–46]`

- [ ] Same voice, style and font standards as external work
- [ ] Patterson Priorities article is about 300 words and covers who/what/where/when/how
- [ ] Submitted to Corporate Communications by the Friday before publication
- [ ] Email signature unchanged from the standard — no photo, no quote, no social icons, no ads
- [ ] Secondary signature graphic is informational, static, timely, and within 1300 × 300px

## Integrity

- [ ] Every claim, statistic and date is sourced — nothing invented
- [ ] Product and business-unit names spelled and spaced exactly (see **copy-style-guide**)
- [ ] Veterinary terminology correct where applicable — *patient*, *exam room*, *clinic*/*hospital*
