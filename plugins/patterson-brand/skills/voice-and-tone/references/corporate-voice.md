# Corporate brand voice — full reference

Source: **Patterson Companies Brand Guide 2025**, pages 10–12 `[BG25]`.

---

> [!NOTE]
> All example pairs below are the guide's own. The ✅ and ❌ marks reproduce Patterson's published
> correct/incorrect examples verbatim — they are quoted content, not decoration.

> "At our core, we communicate in a positive, customer-first style that is **uncluttered, friendly and
> authentic**."

Four named attributes: **Optimistic · Clear · Truthful · Personable.**

## 1. Optimistic

Rewrite negatives to make them positive. Focus on solutions, not problems.

| | Example |
|---|---|
| ✅ | *Rest assured your SDS requirements are covered.* |
| ❌ | *What keeps you up at night?* |

## 2. Clear

- Declutter your writing. Remove unnecessary prepositional phrases, "ands" and "thats."
- Avoid meaningless words such as **great, special, really, very**.
- Use fewer words. **One thought per sentence.**
- Find the right word instead of using adverbs.
- **Do not use Inc. or Co. in content.**
- Calls to action: **preferably one, never more than three.** Acceptable CTA forms include a phone
  number, a URL, *Contact your rep*, *Contact customer service*.
- Site directions: keep it simple. **Italicize directions, use angle brackets ( > ), bold action
  words.**

| | Example |
|---|---|
| ✅ | *Learn more at pattersonvet.com > equipment > finance > Get Prequalified* |
| ❌ | *To learn more, go to pattersonvet.com, then click "Equipment" then click "Finance" then click "Get Prequalified."* |
| ✅ CTA block | `1.800.000.0000` / `pattersondental.com` |
| ❌ CTA block | Four or more stacked CTAs |

## 3. Truthful

> "Because we are a company teeming with helpful people, we write authentically. Avoid using internal
> jargon and focus on the way our customers feel. We communicate **with** our customer, not **at**
> them, which builds our credibility and trust."

| | Example |
|---|---|
| ✅ | *Learn how a team approach gets everyone on the same page when you enroll your team in Communication and Service continuing education.* |
| ❌ | *WHAT IF… Your employees all embraced quality service and communication? GETTING THEM ALL ON THE SAME PAGE IS THE PLACE TO START!* |

## 4. Personable

Ask: *Does it sound like a caring person wrote it? Is the level of formality correct?*

| | Example |
|---|---|
| ✅ | *If you haven't had the opportunity to explore Kerr AlgiNot Alginate Alternative, you're in for a treat. With faster setup, cleaner cleanup, plus plenty of clinical upside, this nontraditional option could be the right next step for your office team.* |
| ❌ | *ALIGNOT? HOW 'BOUT ALGIYES!!! Get ready for Kerr AlgiNot Alginate Alternative. It's got faster setup, cleans up well, and more! Try it TODAY in your office!* |

## 5. Style baseline `[BG25 p.11]`

- **Follow AP style. For medical articles, follow AMA style.**
- **Use the active voice, not passive.**

| | Example |
|---|---|
| ✅ | *Keep current with continuing education courses from Patterson Veterinary Academy.* |
| ❌ | *Learnings can be had when you sign up for continuing education through Patterson Veterinary Academy.* |
| ✅ | `12 a.m. – 1 p.m.` |
| ❌ | `12 A.M. - 1 P.M.` |

## 6. Digital writing `[BG25 p.11–12]`

- Good content is user-friendly and accessible. **Use the words your customers use.**
- People skim digital communication. **Make your content easily scannable.**
- Use clear headlines and subheads that communicate the intent of the content.

| | Example |
|---|---|
| ✅ | *Our continuing education will inspire you with ideas for better client service and growing your practice.* |
| ❌ | *Veterinary-specific education delivered by experts will educate and inspire you with new ideas and best practices to strengthen the business. You'll receive the know-how, the tools, and the confidence to better meet the needs of pet…* |

## 7. What the voice sits on

| Element | Text |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | *We are Patterson. We Strengthen the People Who Keep Us and Our Animals Healthy.* |
| **Vision** | *We will be the most indispensable partner for animal and oral health professionals, guiding them with bold solutions and a personal touch.* |
| **Values** | *We are Passionate. We are Focused. We are People-First. We are Always Advancing.* |
| **Brand equities** `[BG25 p.7]` | Identical across every business unit: Service Excellence · Unrivaled Support · Dedicated People · Easy & Convenient · Business Solutions |
| **Brand promise** | *Trusted Expertise. Unrivaled Support.®* |

Purpose, Vision and Values are at `[BG25 p.4–5]`.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Running copy takes a comma and no ®; designed graphics take periods and ®, once per piece
> `[BG25 p.6, p.28]`. The promise is not to be used as a headline except in broad, whole-company
> pieces. The Purpose/Vision/Values do **not** replace the promise and must not be used in the same
> way `[BG25 p.5]`.

## 9. Internal communications `[BG25 p.43–46]`

> "When crafting all messaging, internal communications need to follow the same Patterson standards of
> voice, style and font selection."

**Patterson Priorities** — the weekly all-employee e-newsletter, sent **every Thursday**.

- Articles run **about 300 words**.
- Include visuals where available, and a link if needed, to enhance engagement.
- Draft "a few, simple lines, including **who, what, where, when and how**".
- Email a draft to Corporate Communications with an ideal publication date. **Deadline: the Friday
  prior to publication.**

Channels: corporate email campaigns, Patterson Priorities, news features on Inside/Patterson, and
one-off requests.

## 10. Email `[BG25 p.28–32, p.58]`

**Email marketing.** *"We engage our customer by sending relevant information to them, with subscriber
lists segmented by interest."* Hero-spot headlines, subject lines, preheader text and subheads are
**all sentence case**.

**Email signatures.** *"As an extension of our brand, they are not intended to reflect your personal
style or views, but rather project the Patterson brand."*

```text
Employee Name, RDH | Employee Title, Department | Patterson Dental | pattersondental.com
(w) 651.000.0000 | (m) 612.000.0000 | employee.name@pattersondental.com

TRUSTED EXPERTISE. UNRIVALED SUPPORT.®
```

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Name | Arial **Bold**, 10 pt, navy ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-003767-003767) R:0 G:55 B:103 |
| Credentials (e.g. RDH) | Arial **Bold** — allowed only if they pertain to your job function and add credibility |
| Title, Department | Arial Regular, 10 pt |
| Business unit | Arial **Bold**, 10 pt |
| URL, phone, email | Arial Regular, 10 pt |
| Brand promise line | Arial **Bold**, **all caps**, 8 pt, sky blue ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-00A8E1-00A8E1) R:0 G:168 B:225 |

> [!WARNING]
> **Never:** a photo of yourself; multiple colors, fonts or sizes; a stacked signature; inspirational
> quotes; social media icons; ads or announcements.

**Secondary signatures** `[BG25 p.29–32]` — an optional custom footer graphic **above** the signature.
**Informational, not promotional** — *"The email signature should not be considered a marketing
channel."* Suitable: holiday shipping reminders, trade shows, local events, weather impacts. Must be
static, designed by the business unit creative team, timely, and no wider than 1300px or taller than
300px.
