# Patterson Dental marketing voice — full reference

Source: **Patterson Dental Marketing Copy Voice**, updated **9/25/18** `[VOICE18]`. The document's own
subtitle is *"An addendum to the Patterson Brand Guide."*

---

## 1. Scope — this is a layer, not a replacement

`[VOICE18]` **adds to** the corporate voice ([`corporate-voice.md`](corporate-voice.md)). Everything
in the corporate voice still applies to Dental copy: optimistic, clear, truthful, personable; AP style;
active voice; sentence case.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Do not swap one for the other, and do not blend Dental attributes into corporate or Veterinary copy.

> "When we describe the voice of our marketing copy, we are not talking specifically about the
> messages we send or the words we are choosing. Instead, we are talking about a set of ideals that we
> espouse as we write for the dental business… We need to continue showing up as the same company
> regardless of who is sitting behind the keyboard."

## 2. The three attributes

> **"If our copy is empathetic, empowering and authoritative, we will always be on brand."**

### Empathetic

> "We not only understand what the customer is talking about, we have the insider knowledge to feel
> what they are feeling."

Written for doctors, office managers, hygienists, assistants and others. Solutions are offered from a
place of unadulterated authenticity.

> [!TIP]
> **Guardrail:** emotional connection is good; overselling is bad.

### Empowering

Every piece should make the customer think about the next step toward actualising their vision.
Aspirational language, grounded in the facts of running a business.

> [!TIP]
> **Guardrail:** nudge — do not shove — people toward purchases that can change their practice
> forever.

### Authoritative

The healthcare professionals we address are experts in their field. At Patterson we are experts on the
business practices, equipment, technology, software and merchandise a dental team needs.

> "We leverage our 140+ years in business to speak with a high level of authority regarding the
> efficacy of our product offerings (and our advice)."

> [!TIP]
> **Guardrail:** *"There is no reason to be shy."*

## 3. Positioning line

> "Patterson Dental is relied upon for expertise and is trusted to support dental practices around the
> country as they endeavor to grow both in size and capacity for care."

## 6. Audience terminology reminder

Dental and Veterinary do not share vocabulary `[COPY22]`:

| Veterinary copy uses | Not |
|---|---|
| patient | pet, animal |
| exam room | operatory |
| clinic *or* hospital | office, business |

Sales roles also differ:

| Unit | Title |
|---|---|
| Dental | **territory representatives** |
| Veterinary | **territory managers** |
