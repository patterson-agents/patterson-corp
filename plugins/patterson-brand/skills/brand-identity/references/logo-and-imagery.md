> replace the brand promise and must not be used in the same manner.

## 5. Placement `[BG25 p.15, p.17]`

Primary: lower right corner or upper left. Where impractical in digital, centre in a narrow space or
use the upper left in menus and banners. Square logo: lower right, upper left, bleed top/bottom, or
float.

## 6. Co-branding `[BG25 p.18]`

A context where Patterson has ownership, partial ownership, alliance or partnership with a product or
service. **Patterson must always visually remain the dominant brand and always sits on the left.**
Partner logo on the right, proportionally **slightly smaller**. Separated by a vertical rule (1 pt for
a 3.5"-wide logo). Horizontal format preferred; vertical acceptable when necessary.

> [!NOTE]
> Conflict: the 2022 vendor guide states the partner logo should be **"25% smaller"**. `[BG25]`
> softens this to "proportionally slightly smaller" — but 25% remains the only numeric guidance in
> existence.

## 7. Unique branding `[BG25 p.21]`

- **Brand entities** — the Patterson logo associated with a niche market pursuit; the Patterson brand
  paired with a text-only treatment (e.g. "Education Center").
- **Sponsorship branding** — Patterson shares the page with another brand without reading as a
  co-brand. Logo proximity is flexible as long as they are spatially separated; clarifying text (e.g.
  "Sponsored by Patterson Dental") helps.

## 8. Prohibited `[BG25 p.20, p.23]`

> [!WARNING]
> **Never:** use the old gold and blue logo; use an old version of the logo; resize any part of the
> logo; distort or skew; rearrange elements; add names or elements; alter color; place the logo
> directly over an image; place an image inside the shape.
>
> For AHI additionally: never separate the graphic from the logo; never place the logo over a bright
> color background.

## 9. Favicons `[BG25 p.48–49]`

The Patterson logo is a protected asset; changes are not allowed. **Exception:** the hands-only favicon
may be modified (color or background) on social media only, under **all** of:

- [ ] Change made by the corporate marketing team, on social platforms only.
- [ ] Must coincide with a **month-long observance** agreed in advance by leadership including business
      unit leadership.
- [ ] Business partners must commit to a steady content cadence for the whole month.
- [ ] **Maximum twice per year**; never for one-off events (e.g. Earth Day).
- [ ] Never for customer marketing campaigns; never shared outside corporate marketing.

Documented examples: Facebook header for Black History Month; LinkedIn header for Pride Month.

## 10. Photography `[BG25 p.33–39]`

Tone must map to at least one Patterson **value**. Depict clean, healthy people, animals and
environments and innovative products and technology. Crop artfully to focus on the subject.

| Category | Value | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Product `[p.34]` | Focused | Good lighting, soft shadows. **Web: individual products on a white sweep.** Multiple products: clean, contemporary, minimal contrasting backgrounds, shallow depth of field |
| Interaction `[p.35]` | People-First | Good exposure, lots of natural light. Shallow DoF. Authentic, not clichéd or staged. Clothing must accurately represent a real-life setting |
| Patient/procedural `[p.36]` | Always Advancing | Actual scenarios, not staged. High exposure, natural light, shallow DoF. Production animals must be clean — **no mud or flies** |
| Technology/equipment `[p.37]` | Always Advancing | Shallow DoF. Shot in the actual environment or on a white **or gray** sweep. Bright, well lit with highlight lighting |
| Employees `[p.39]` | Passionate | High exposure, natural light, shallow DoF. Actual work environments. Must not look staged |

### Dental model regulatory requirements `[BG25 p.38]`

> [!CAUTION]
> All items appearing in a shoot (scrubs, masks, etc.) must be **Patterson products**. For operatory
> and procedural photos: hair pulled back if length requires; mask completely covering **nose, mouth
> and chin** — not under the chin, hanging from one ear, or worn as a headband; eye protection,
> **tightly fitted** gloves and scrubs; **no skin showing on chest, arms or wrists**; patients wear a
> patient bib and eye protection. A **Patterson Photo and Video Model Release form** is required — URL
> in [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md).

## 11. Icons `[BG25 p.41–42]`, `[IMG20 p.2]`

Simple, modern, **linear style**; all created to the same guidelines. **Icons must never overpower a
piece and should not be the main visual** — they are small supporting cues. Sets exist for Animal
Health/Veterinary, Dental and General. **Over 300 icons** are available from the corporate icon library
(SharePoint — see [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md)).

> [!WARNING]
> Never: use colors outside the brand palette; place icons on backgrounds that are too dark, poorly
> contrasted, or carry photographic detail that conflicts with the art.

Export:

| Target | Spec |
|---|---|
| Web | Vector SVG at 80 × 80px (code scales per pattern) |
| Email | PNG at 80 × 80px @2x |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Export SVG from the **.RGB** icon file — do not convert the CMYK file to RGB and export, as it
> produces the incorrect Digital SkyBlue color. Illustrator recommended.

## 13. Responsive imagery `[BG25 p.61]`

Images must be visually effective when cropped for desktop/tablet/phone in landscape or portrait. The
reference rendering context is a **Kentico CTA module**.

| Viewport | Crop behaviour |
|---|---|
| Desktop horizontal | Full width, cropped top and bottom |
| Mobile horizontal | Full height, cropped left and right |
| Mobile vertical | Original proportions, scalable |
