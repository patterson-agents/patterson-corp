## 8. Usage rules

- Icons must **never** use colors outside the brand palette `[BG25 p.42]`.
- Buttons may use palette colors other than sky "as long as the button stands out in the context of
  the design" `[BG25 p.57]`.
- Video full-frame grounds `[BG25 p.52]`:

  | Unit | Ground |
  |---|---|
  | Patterson Dental | ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-00A8E1-00A8E1) sky `#00A8E1` |
  | Patterson Veterinary | ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-003767-003767) navy `#003767` |
  | Patterson Companies (corporate) and AHI | ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-FFFFFF-FFFFFF) white `#FFFFFF` |

- QR codes: keep the pixel field a single color, preferably black `[BG25 p.51]`.
- Never alter logo color; never place the logo over an image `[BG25 p.20]`.

## 9. Measured contrast (computed here, not published by Patterson)

| Combination | Ratio | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| White on sky `#00A8E1` | ≈ **2.3:1** | Fails WCAG AA at every text size |
| White on digital sky `#269BCB` | ≈ **3.4:1** | Passes AA for large text and non-text UI components only |
