# Patterson visual brand review checklist

Work top to bottom.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Cite the rule and the source for every failure; do not invent rules.

---

## Case — check this first, it fails most often

- [ ] Headlines are **sentence case** `[BG25 p.59]`
- [ ] Subheads and secondary subheads are sentence case `[BG25 p.59]`
- [ ] Body copy and captions are sentence case `[BG25 p.59]`
- [ ] **Button / CTA labels are sentence case, never all caps** `[BG25 p.57]`
- [ ] Eyebrows and text links are sentence case `[DS20 p.10]`
- [ ] Any all-caps present is one of the three sanctioned exceptions only: print titles
      `[BG25 p.25]`, footer/title text `[BG25 p.27]`, email-signature promise line `[BG25 p.28]`

## Color

- [ ] Every hex used appears in the Patterson palette ([`palette.csv`](palette.csv)) — no off-palette
      colors
- [ ] Body copy is ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-58585B-58585B) `#58585B`, not navy and not black
      `[BG25 p.24]`
- [ ] Eyebrow text is teal ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-00817D-00817D) `#00817D` — **not** sky
      blue `[DS20 p.16]`, `[DPL]`
- [ ] Default button fill is sky ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-00A8E1-00A8E1) `#00A8E1` with white
      text, or another palette color that still stands out in context `[BG25 p.57]`
- [ ] Tertiary colors are used only for infographics/presentations `[BG25 p.24]`
- [ ] Secondary colors do not dominate the primary palette `[BG25 p.24]`
- [ ] Accent colors are **not** used for body copy or legal disclaimers `[DS20 p.8]`
- [ ] Tints, if used, come from the 75/50/25 ramp — not an invented ramp `[BG25 p.24]`
- [ ] Icons use no color outside the palette `[BG25 p.42]`
- [ ] If the piece is digital and mixes `#00A8E1` with `#269BCB`, the two-palette conflict is
      acknowledged rather than silently resolved

## Typography

- [ ] Face is Proxima Nova, or Arial in an OfficeSuite context `[BG25 p.25]`
- [ ] No lookalike substitute (Figtree, Montserrat, etc.) has been introduced
- [ ] Headlines are Extrabold; subheads Semibold; body Regular or Light; CTA Bold `[BG25 p.25, p.27]`
- [ ] Tracking is −10 (`-0.01em`), or −25 at large scale; body 0 to −10 `[BG25 p.27]`
- [ ] Heading leading is ~75% of type size; body leading is 125–150% `[BG25 p.27]`
- [ ] Type is flush left `[BG25 p.27]`
- [ ] No `@font-face` rule and no font binary is being shipped

> [!CAUTION]
> A committed font binary or an `@font-face` rule for Proxima Nova is a licensing exposure, not a
> style nit. Report it as the highest severity.

## Logo

- [ ] Correct mark for the audience — handshake-only is corporate communications only `[BG25 p.13]`
- [ ] Colorway is navy, sky, dark gray, black or white reverse `[BG25 p.14]`
- [ ] Clear space: full "P" height in print, half in digital `[BG25 p.14]`
- [ ] Placement is lower right or upper left `[BG25 p.15]`
- [ ] Not over an image, not recolored, not distorted, not rearranged, nothing added `[BG25 p.20]`
- [ ] Co-branded: Patterson on the **left** and dominant; partner slightly smaller, vertical rule
      between `[BG25 p.18]`
- [ ] Secondary/square logo ≤ ~20% of a vertical page width, ≤ ~15% horizontal `[BG25 p.17]`

## Geometry and controls

- [ ] Corner radius is 5px `[BG25 p.57]`
- [ ] Button height 46px; 30px padding left and right of the label `[BG25 p.57]`
- [ ] Spacing lands on the 5px grid `[DPL]`
- [ ] Icons are 80 × 80 `[IMG20 p.2]`
- [ ] No invented shadow scale — Patterson publishes none

## Copy mechanics inside the design

- [ ] No punctuation in button labels `[DS20 p.10]`
- [ ] No phone number inside a button — phone numbers use text-link styling `[DS20 p.10]`
- [ ] No CTA phrased as a question `[DS20 p.10]`
- [ ] One CTA, never more than three `[BG25 p.10]`
- [ ] Brand promise: comma and no ® in running copy; periods and ® in designed graphics, once per
      piece `[BG25 p.6, p.28]`
- [ ] No "Inc." or "Co." in content `[BG25 p.10]`

## Imagery

- [ ] Tone maps to at least one Patterson value `[BG25 p.33]`
- [ ] Natural light, shallow depth of field, authentic rather than staged `[BG25 p.35]`
- [ ] Dental operatory shots meet PPE requirements — mask over nose/mouth/chin, eye protection,
      fitted gloves, no skin on chest/arms/wrists, patient bib and eye protection `[BG25 p.38]`
- [ ] Production animals are clean — no mud, no flies `[BG25 p.36]`
- [ ] Image survives cropping to desktop, tablet and phone in both orientations `[BG25 p.61]`
