# OfficeSuite — Outlook, PowerPoint, Teams, SharePoint

---

## 1. Typeface `[BG25 p.25]`

**Arial** is the sanctioned OfficeSuite face. Arial Regular for body; Arial Bold for headlines and
subheads. **Arial Nova Light** is available as an extra weight where it is installed.

> [!CAUTION]
> Proxima Nova is the brand face for design and web, licensed through Adobe Fonts. It is not installed
> on a typical Patterson machine, and its licence does not permit embedding a font file in a document.
> A deck or document that uses it will reflow for other people. Use Arial.

## 2. Email signatures `[BG25 p.28]`

> "As an extension of our brand, they are not intended to reflect your personal style or views, but
> rather project the Patterson brand."

```text
Employee Name, RDH | Employee Title, Department | Patterson Dental | pattersondental.com
(w) 651.000.0000 | (m) 612.000.0000 | employee.name@pattersondental.com

TRUSTED EXPERTISE. UNRIVALED SUPPORT.®
```

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Name | Arial **Bold**, 10 pt |
| Credentials (e.g. RDH) | Arial **Bold** — allowed **only** if they pertain to your job function and add credibility |
| Title, Department | Arial Regular, 10 pt |
| Business unit | Arial **Bold**, 10 pt |
| URL, phone, email | Arial Regular, 10 pt |
| Brand promise line | Arial **Bold**, **all caps**, 8 pt |
| Name color | ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-003767-003767) Navy blue, R:0 G:55 B:103 |
| Brand promise color | ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-00A8E1-00A8E1) Sky blue, R:0 G:168 B:225 |

> [!WARNING]
> **Never:** use a photo of yourself; use multiple colors, different fonts or various sizes; stack the
> signature (it takes too much vertical space); include inspirational quotes; include social media
> icons; include ads or announcements.

The registration mark changed from ™ to **®** in 2023 `[BG25 p.28]`. The signature promise line is one
of the only three sanctioned all-caps treatments in the 2025 system.

## 3. Secondary email signatures `[BG25 p.29–32]`

An optional custom footer graphic placed **above** the signature.

- **Informational in nature, not promotional.** *"The email signature should not be considered a
  marketing channel."* Suitable subjects: holiday shipping reminders, trade shows, local events,
  weather impacts.
- Designed by the business unit creative team, following brand standards.
- **Static, not interactive.**
- Timely — do not send holiday shipping information in February.
- Art no wider than **1300px**, no taller than **300px** `[BG25 p.30]`. The recommended working width
  is **750px** `[BG25 p.44]`.

## 4. Internal communications surfaces `[BG25 p.43–46]`

> "When crafting all messaging, internal communications need to follow the same Patterson standards of
> voice, style and font selection."

**Patterson Priorities** — the weekly all-employee e-newsletter, sent **every Thursday**.

- Articles run **about 300 words**.
- Include visuals where available and a link if needed.
- Draft "a few, simple lines, including who, what, where, when and how."
- Email a draft to Corporate Communications with an ideal publication date. **Deadline: the Friday
  prior to publication.**

| Surface | Optimal size | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Internal email banner (mass email, built in Word) | 1300px wide | jpg or png |
| Secondary signature graphic | 750px wide (max 1300 × 300) | jpg or png |
| Patterson Priorities newsletter | 1920 × 1004 (social ratios fine; square OK) | jpg or png |
| Inside/Patterson upper banner, full width | 1920 × 1080, responsive, crops to as small as 1920 × 250 | jpg or png |
| Inside/Patterson "What's New" | 1920 × 1004 | jpg or png |

## 5. Copy mechanics that bite in Office documents

- **Sentence case** for titles, headlines, subheads, body and captions `[BG25 p.59]`; email subject
  lines and preheader text too `[BG25 p.58]`.
- Ampersands: not a substitute for *and*, but **permitted in PowerPoint titles and bullets to save
  space** — be consistent within the deck `[COPY22]`.
- Phone numbers use **periods**, not hyphens `[COPY22]`.
- Ranges use an **en dash with spaces**: `6:30 – 7 p.m.` `[COPY22]`.
- **No `Inc.` or `Co.`** in content `[BG25 p.10]`.
- Department names: *the Finance department*, *the Human Resources team* — unit capitalized, the word
  *department*/*team* not `[COPY22]`.
- Job titles capitalized only before a name `[COPY22]`.
- Memo `TO:` lines have preferred recipient terms — see **copy-style-guide**
  `references/mechanics.md` §12.

## 6. Accessibility

`[BG25 p.26]` gives audible reading technology as a reason for the sentence-case rule, and
`[BG25 p.62]` cites ADA standards for banner ads. In an Office context that means: sentence case, real
heading styles rather than manually enlarged text, alt text on images, and enough contrast.

> [!WARNING]
> White on sky ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-00A8E1-00A8E1) `#00A8E1` measures ~2.3:1 and fails
> WCAG AA at any size.
