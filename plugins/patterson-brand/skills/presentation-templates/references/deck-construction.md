# Building a Patterson deck

Every rule below carries its source. Where Patterson has not published something, it is written
`[TBD]` rather than filled in.

---

> [!CAUTION]
> **Do not invent layout names, slide sizes or master names.**

## 1. Before you build

1. Get the official template from SharePoint (see [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md)). It is the
   governed artifact; this document is only the ruleset behind it.
2. Confirm the business unit — Dental, Veterinary, Animal Health International or corporate. It
   changes the logo family `[BG25 p.19]`, the emphasis color on full-frame slides `[BG25 p.52]`, the
   voice layer (see **voice-and-tone**) and the sales-role terminology (see **copy-style-guide**).
3. Confirm the audience — internal or customer-facing. Internal work follows the same standards
   `[BG25 p.43]`.

## 2. Slide roles, and what each one is allowed to do

> [!NOTE]
> These are derived from documented Brand Guide rules, **not** from a published Patterson layout
> inventory.

### Title slide

- Deck title in Arial Bold, **sentence case** `[BG25 p.59]`.
- Ground: white, or navy ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-003767-003767) `#003767` as a full-frame
  emphasis ground `[BG25 p.52]`.
- Logo lower right or upper left, with clear space of half the "P" height `[BG25 p.14–15]`.
- The brand promise may appear here as a sign-off — **once per deck** `[BG25 p.6]`. Designed form:
  *Trusted Expertise. Unrivaled Support.®*

### Section divider

- Navy ground with white reverse type is the documented emphasis treatment `[BG25 p.52]`,
  `[BG25 p.14]`.
- Short, sentence-case section name. If you want a small label above it, that is the eyebrow pattern:
  **teal ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-00817D-00817D) `#00817D`, sentence case, short**
  `[DS20 p.16]` — not sky blue and not uppercase.

### Content slide

- White ground `[BG25 p.24]`; headline in navy, body in
  ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-58585B-58585B) `#58585B`.
- One idea per slide; **one thought per sentence** `[BG25 p.10]`.
- Bullets in Arial Regular, sentence case.
- Ampersands are permitted in titles and bullets to save space, if used consistently `[COPY22]`.

### Data / infographic slide

- Use the **tertiary palette**, which `[BG25 p.24]` designates for exactly this.
- A five-series ramp matching the shipped theme:

  ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-003767-003767) `#003767` ·
  ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-00A8E1-00A8E1) `#00A8E1` ·
  ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-7BC24D-7BC24D) `#7BC24D` ·
  ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-00817D-00817D) `#00817D` ·
  ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-522E91-522E91) `#522E91`

- Tints (75/50/25) shade a series without leaving the palette `[BG25 p.24]`.
- Accent colors must **never** carry body copy or legal disclaimers `[DS20 p.8]`.
- Never introduce an off-palette color to distinguish a series. If you run out, use tints.

### Closing / CTA slide

- **One CTA. Never more than three** `[BG25 p.10]`. Acceptable forms: a phone number, a URL, *Contact
  your rep*, *Contact customer service*.
- Phone numbers use periods: `800.873.7683` `[COPY22]`.
- URLs lowercase, no `http://` `[COPY22]`.
- CTA text is sentence case and is never a question `[DS20 p.10]`.

## 3. Type

| Element | Face | Case |
|---|---|---|
| Slide title | Arial Bold | Sentence case `[BG25 p.59]` |
| Subhead | Arial Bold | Sentence case |
| Body, bullets | Arial Regular | Sentence case |
| Running footer / deck title in the footer | Arial Bold | May be all caps `[BG25 p.27]` |

Arial is the sanctioned OfficeSuite face `[BG25 p.25]`. Arial Nova Light is available as an extra
weight where installed.

> [!CAUTION]
> Do not use Proxima Nova in a deck and do not embed a font file.

> [!NOTE]
> Where a print/design layout spec is needed, the underlying brand values are: tracking −10, leading
> 75% of size for headings and 125–150% for body, flush left `[BG25 p.27]`. PowerPoint expresses these
> differently, so treat them as intent rather than as literal settings.

## 4. Imagery in decks

- Photography must be authentic, well exposed, naturally lit, shallow depth of field, and its tone
  must map to at least one Patterson value `[BG25 p.33]`.
- Product shots for screen sit on a **white sweep**; equipment may use white or gray
  `[BG25 p.34, p.37]`.
- Production animals must be clean — no mud, no flies `[BG25 p.36]`.
- Icons: linear style, 80 × 80px, supporting cue only, never the main visual, never off-palette
  `[BG25 p.41–42]`, `[IMG20 p.2]`.
- Never place the logo over an image `[BG25 p.20]`.

> [!CAUTION]
> Dental operatory imagery must meet the PPE requirements — mask over nose, mouth and chin; eye
> protection; fitted gloves; no skin on chest, arms or wrists; patient bib and eye protection
> `[BG25 p.38]`.

Documented image sizes that decks commonly reuse:

| Surface | Size | Format | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Video canvas / full-bleed still | 1920 × 1080 @ 72dpi | png, ai, layered psd | `[BG25 p.52]` |
| Internal email banner | 1300px wide | jpg or png | `[BG25 p.44]` |
| Patterson Priorities newsletter image | 1920 × 1004 | jpg or png | `[BG25 p.44]` |
| Inside/Patterson banner | 1920 × 1080, crops to 1920 × 250 | jpg or png | `[BG25 p.45]` |
| Social landscape / square (if the deck feeds social) | 1200 × 628 / 1080 × 1080 | png for graphics, jpg for photos | `[BG25 p.48]` |

`[TBD: no slide-size or aspect-ratio standard (16:9 vs 4:3) is published in any extracted Patterson
source. Take it from the official template.]`

## 5. Video inside a deck `[BG25 p.52–53]`

Canvas 1920 × 1080 at 72dpi. Preferred formats png, ai, layered psd; deliver graphics with layers or
an alpha channel. Generic graphic opens run 2–3 seconds with a half-second transition.

Full-frame grounds by unit:

| Unit | Ground |
|---|---|
| Dental | ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-00A8E1-00A8E1) sky `#00A8E1` |
| Veterinary | ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-003767-003767) navy `#003767` |
| Corporate and AHI | ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-FFFFFF-FFFFFF) white `#FFFFFF` |

The logo occupies the middle third of the frame.

## 6. Governance

> `[DS20 p.40]`: *"Every time you override a global component in one area of a product, you erode the
> consistency of your design system."*

The same applies to a deck — compose from the template's existing layouts instead of building bespoke
slides.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Graphical treatment of the Purpose/Vision/Values requires Corporate Marketing and Communications;
> there is **no single PVV logo** `[BG25 p.5]`. If a deck needs a designed PVV slide, route it rather
> than composing one.

## 7. Review checklist

- [ ] Built from the official SharePoint template, or the deviation is stated
- [ ] Arial throughout; no Proxima Nova, no embedded font file
- [ ] Every title, headline, subhead, bullet and caption is sentence case
- [ ] Any all-caps is a footer/title treatment only
- [ ] Every color is in the Patterson palette; charts use the tertiary/infographic ramp
- [ ] Body copy is `#58585B`; headlines navy
- [ ] Eyebrow-style labels are teal and sentence case
- [ ] Logo colorway, placement and clear space correct; not over an image
- [ ] Co-branding: Patterson left and dominant
- [ ] Brand promise appears at most once, in the right form for its context
- [ ] One CTA (max three); phone numbers with periods; no CTA phrased as a question
- [ ] No `Inc.` or `Co.`
- [ ] Ampersand usage consistent across the deck
- [ ] Imagery meets category and PPE rules
- [ ] Icons 80 × 80, supporting only, on-palette
