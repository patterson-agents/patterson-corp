# Starting outline — Patterson deck

A neutral skeleton derived from documented Brand Guide rules. Replace every `<…>` placeholder.
Everything is sentence case.

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This is not a reproduction of the official Patterson template** — start from the SharePoint
> template where you can (see [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md)).

## 1. Title slide

- **Ground:** white, or navy ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-003767-003767) `#003767` for emphasis
  `[BG25 p.52]`
- **Title (Arial Bold, sentence case):** `<Deck title — one clear idea>`
- **Subtitle (Arial Regular):** `<Audience, date, presenter>`
- **Logo:** lower right or upper left; clear space = half the "P" height `[BG25 p.14–15]`
- **Optional sign-off, once per deck only:** Trusted Expertise. Unrivaled Support.® `[BG25 p.6]`

## 2. Agenda / what we'll cover

- Arial Regular bullets, sentence case, one thought each
- Three to five items; if there are more, the deck is doing two jobs

## 3. Section divider

- **Ground:** navy ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-003767-003767) `#003767`, type in white reverse
  `[BG25 p.14, p.52]`
- **Optional label above the section name:** teal ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-00817D-00817D)
  `#00817D`, sentence case, short — the eyebrow pattern `[DS20 p.16]`. Not sky blue, not uppercase.
- **Section name (Arial Bold, sentence case):** `<Section>`

## 4. Content slide (repeat as needed)

- **Headline (navy, Arial Bold, sentence case):** `<The takeaway, not the topic>`
- **Body** — ![](https://img.shields.io/badge/-58585B-58585B) `#58585B`, Arial Regular: three to five
  bullets, one thought per sentence
- **Optional supporting icon:** 80 × 80, linear, on-palette, never the main visual `[BG25 p.41]`
- **Optional image:** authentic, naturally lit, shallow depth of field, tone mapped to a Patterson
  value `[BG25 p.33]`

## 5. Data slide

- **Headline:** `<What the data shows>` — state the conclusion, not "Q3 results"
- **Series colors, in order** `[BG25 p.24]`:

  | # | Swatch | Hex |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/-003767-003767" alt="003767"> | `#003767` navy |
  | 2 | <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/-00A8E1-00A8E1" alt="00A8E1"> | `#00A8E1` sky |
  | 3 | <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/-7BC24D-7BC24D" alt="7BC24D"> | `#7BC24D` green |
  | 4 | <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/-00817D-00817D" alt="00817D"> | `#00817D` teal |
  | 5 | <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/-522E91-522E91" alt="522E91"> | `#522E91` purple |

- Beyond five series, use the 75/50/25 tints of the same colors — never an off-palette color
- Axis and label text in `#58585B`; accent colors never carry body copy or disclaimers `[DS20 p.8]`

## 6. Proof / customer slide

- Quote or testimonial in sentence case, attributed correctly
- Veterinary audiences: *patient*, *exam room*, *clinic*/*hospital* `[COPY22]`
- Dental copy carries the empathetic / empowering / authoritative layer — see **voice-and-tone**

## 7. Close

- **One CTA. Never more than three** `[BG25 p.10]`
- Acceptable forms: a phone number (periods, e.g. `800.873.7683`), a URL (lowercase, no `http://`),
  *Contact your rep*, *Contact customer service*
- CTA text is sentence case and never a question `[DS20 p.10]`

## 8. Optional appendix

- Sources, methodology, detail tables
- Same type and color rules apply
