# Patterson copy style

Base references: **The Associated Press Stylebook** and **Webster's New World College Dictionary**
`[COPY22]`. For medical articles, follow **AMA style** `[BG25 p.11]`. Patterson's documented
exceptions to AP override AP.

Source keys: `[BG25 p.N]` Brand Guide 2025 · `[COPY22]` Copy Style Guide 2022 · `[DS20 p.N]`
DesignSystem_042120 · `[SOC19]` Social Media Writing Guidelines.

---

## 1. Sentence case — check this before anything else

Headlines, subheads, body copy, captions, buttons, text links, eyebrows, email subject lines and
preheader text are **all sentence case** `[BG25 p.57, p.58, p.59]`, `[DS20 p.10]`. All-caps in a
digital channel reads as shouting and degrades screen readers `[BG25 p.25–26]`.

Only three all-caps survivors: print titles `[BG25 p.25]`, footer/title text `[BG25 p.27]`, and the
brand-promise line in an Outlook email signature `[BG25 p.28]`.

> [!WARNING]
> **The 2022 guide's "Titles should be all caps" is superseded** — if a draft came from a pre-2025
> template, expect this error.

## 2. Exceptions to AP — memorize these five

| Item | Patterson rule | AP would say |
|---|---|---|
| Academic degrees | **No periods:** BA, PhD, RDH, DDS, DVM, CPA | periods |
| Brackets | Use **brackets** to clarify inside quoted material | parentheses |
| Cents | Dollar sign and decimal: `$.06`, `$.50` | cent sign |
| Italics | Italicize book/journal/magazine/newspaper titles and scientific names (*Staphylococcus aureus*) | no italics |
| Serial comma | **No Oxford comma** in a simple series (*red, white and blue*); **do** use it in a complex series for clarity. AMA-style doctor-authored articles may use it | — |

## 3. Numbers, dates, money, time

- Spell out zero–nine; numerals for 10+, except when a number starts a sentence. Always numerals for
  ages, dates, dimensions, percentages, measures and weights.
- **Percent uses the symbol (%)** in copy.
- Decimals under 1 take a leading zero: *increased 0.9 percent*. Fractions under one are spelled and
  hyphenated in body copy: *one-third*.
- Money: `$1M`, `$1B`. Financial copy includes cents (*$0.51*); marketing drops `.00` (*$100*). Never
  *$1 billion dollars*. `K` for money only in tight space; elsewhere `K` means kilometre.
- Dates: *January 1, 2015*; *Wednesday, July 22, 2015*. No month or day abbreviations, no *-st*/*-rd*.
  Numerals-only grids use a four-digit year: *1/1/2015*.
- **En dash with a space on both sides** for ranges, not a hyphen: *6:30 – 7 p.m.*
- Time: drop `:00` on the hour (*6 p.m.*); lowercase with periods and a space (*6:30 p.m.*).
- **Phone numbers use periods, not hyphens**: `505.884.6747`. No leading "1" on toll-free. Comma
  before an extension: `866.234.2165, ext. 61727`.
- Units take a space: `5 ml syringe`, not `5ml`. Use **`mL` for Veterinary** `[COPY22]`.

## 4. Punctuation

One space after a period. Colon before a list only after a complete sentence. Exclamation points
sparingly. Doubles for quotation marks; periods and commas inside. `Q&A`, not `Q & A`. Ampersands are
not a substitute for *and* — permitted only in catalog headers, trademarked vendor names, and
PowerPoint titles/bullets to save space. Asterisks link to fine print, never for emphasis.

## 5. Buttons and CTAs

- **No punctuation in button labels** `[DS20 p.10]`.
- **No phone numbers in buttons** — phone numbers use text-link styling with the "phone" media icon.
- **Never phrase a CTA as a question.**
- Link text for an email address must not display the address itself.
- **One CTA per piece, never more than three** `[BG25 p.10]`.

## 6. Naming — the ones that get missed

> [!CAUTION]
> Product-name spacing is legally critical: **TechEdge**, not *Tech Edge*. Do not recreate a graphic
> treatment — write *Prana*, not *prAna*.

- **No `Inc.` or `Co.` in content** `[BG25 p.10]`.
- **Patterson Animal Health is internal-only.** Customer-facing, say **Patterson Veterinary** or
  **Animal Health International**.
- **Patterson Veterinary** on first reference; *Patterson Vet* is acceptable afterwards in marketing
  copy but **never in legal copy**.
- Veterinary terminology: **patient** (not pet/animal), **exam room** (not operatory), **clinic** or
  **hospital** (not office/business). **Elizabethan collar**, never *e-collar*.
- Brand promise: running copy takes a **comma and no ®** — *Trusted Expertise, Unrivaled Support*.
  Designed graphics take **periods and ®**, once per piece `[BG25 p.6, p.28]`.

## 7. Style baseline

Follow AP; AMA for medical articles. **Use the active voice.** Declutter — remove unnecessary
prepositional phrases, "ands" and "thats". Avoid *great, special, really, very*. **One thought per
sentence.** Find the right word instead of using an adverb. `[BG25 p.10–11]`

Site directions: italicize, use angle brackets, bold the action word —
*Learn more at pattersonvet.com > equipment > finance > Get Prequalified* `[BG25 p.10]`.
Web addresses: no `http://`, lowercase, `www.` may be dropped if the short form still resolves.
