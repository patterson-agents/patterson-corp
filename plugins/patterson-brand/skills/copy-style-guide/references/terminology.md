Source: **Patterson Companies Copy Style Guide, 04/2022** `[COPY22]` unless noted.

---

## 1. Spelling list

```text
appendixes (not appendices)   cancellation           cloud (not Cloud)
codebook                      COVID                  do's and don'ts
email (no hyphen)             e-commerce             e-newsletter
everyday (adj.)               every day (adv.)       fundraising / fundraiser
gray (not grey)               internet (lowercase)   kickoff (n.)
kick-off (adj.)               kick off (v.)          log in / log off (v.)
login / logon (n.)            Mac (not MAC)          online
on-site (not onsite)          set up (v.)            setup (n.)
back up (v.)                  backup (n.)            smartphone
cell phone                    T-shirt                trade show (two words)
voicemail                     web address            webpage
website                       X-ray
```

Hyphenation patterns:

| Pattern | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `-wide` | No hyphen | `companywide`, `nationwide` |
| `-up` nouns | No hyphen | `startup`, `buildup`, `cleanup` |
| `co-` | Hyphenated for people, closed otherwise | `co-owner`, `co-worker`; `copay`, `coexist` |
| `mini-` / `multi-` | No hyphen | — |
| `self-` | Hyphenated | — |
| `bi-` / `semi-` | Unhyphenated | `biannual`, `biennial`, `semiannual`, `biweekly`, `semiweekly` |
| `toward` | US form, no *s* | — |

## 2. Commonly confused

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **assure** | give confidence to a person |
| **ensure** | guarantee an outcome |
| **insure** | cover with insurance |
| **Diplomate** | a board-certified veterinary specialist |
| **Diplomat** | a country's representative |
| **annual** | an event is not annual until its second year — use **inaugural**, never *first annual* |

## 3. Trademarks and product names

- Initial cap only, or all caps if the name is initials: *Ikea*, *AT&T*.
- Medial capitals are permitted (*NaVetor*, *iPod*) but **do not recreate a graphic treatment**:
  write *Prana*, not *prAna*.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Spacing in product and service names is legally critical and must be maintained:** *TechEdge*,
> not *Tech Edge*.

## 4. Company and business-unit naming

- **No `Inc.` or `Co.` in content** `[BG25 p.10]`.
- **Patterson Animal Health — internal use only.** Customer-facing: **Patterson Veterinary** or
  **Animal Health International**.
- **Patterson Veterinary** on first reference; **Patterson Vet** acceptable afterwards in marketing
  content, **but never in legal copy**.
- **National Veterinary Services Group** spelled out on first reference, **NVS** afterwards.
- Also correct: *Inside/Patterson*, *Patterson Shop*.
- Legal entity: Patterson Companies, Inc. Address on all style-guide letterheads: 1031 Mendota
  Heights Road, Saint Paul, MN 55120.

## 5. Job titles by business unit

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Sales-role titles differ by unit and are **not** interchangeable.

| Unit | Title |
|---|---|
| Dental | **territory representatives** |
| Veterinary | **territory managers** |
| Animal Health | **outside sales representatives (OSRs)** and **inside sales representatives (ISRs)** |

Other required titles:

## 6. Veterinary industry terminology

| Use | Not |
|---|---|
| patient | pet, animal |
| exam room | operatory |
| clinic *or* hospital | office, business |

> [!WARNING]
> **Elizabethan collar** — never *e-collar*, which means an electronic (shock) collar.

## 7. Words to avoid `[BG25 p.10]`

Avoid meaningless intensifiers: **great, special, really, very**. Find the right word instead of
reaching for an adverb. Remove unnecessary prepositional phrases, "ands" and "thats". Avoid internal
jargon — write in the words customers use `[BG25 p.11]`.
