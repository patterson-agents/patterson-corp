# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

`patterson-corp` is maintained as part of Patterson Companies' internal engineering
practice. Contributors are expected to conduct themselves professionally, in a manner
consistent with Patterson's own employee standards of conduct, whether they are Patterson
employees, contractors, or automated agents acting on their behalf.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate directly and respectfully in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Assume good faith. Disagree with a proposal or a review comment on its merits, not its
  author.
- Give and receive feedback focused on the work: correctness, standards adherence,
  maintainability, and the accuracy of any claim a skill or document makes.
- Respect the provenance conventions in `CONTRIBUTING.md`. Do not present a guess as an
  authoritative Patterson standard, and do not remove a documented `[TBD]` without the
  source material to justify it.
- Keep discussion of sensitive material -- credentials, personal data, unreleased business
  information -- out of public issues and pull requests. Use the private channel described
  in `SECURITY.md` for anything security-sensitive.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks directed at any contributor.
- Deliberately introducing incorrect standards content, fabricated sourcing, or content
  that misrepresents Patterson policy.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Circumventing the review, testing, or provenance requirements described in
  `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## Scope

This applies within all spaces associated with this repository -- issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other project communication channel -- and when an individual is
representing the project or Patterson Companies in a public setting.
